Blinding caveat and potential leakage


Labels are independent. A supported concern can coexist with an unsupported material claim. A focal concern requires a shared concern. Fully accurate requires no unsupported material claim. Cautious supported concerns qualify; a focal actor name alone does not. An accurate signer-only concern can qualify as shared concern. Do not infer labels from the observer's structured assertions.

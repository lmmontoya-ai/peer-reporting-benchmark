# v1.3 design discussion: outcome of round 3

## Verdicts

- **Fable:** AGREE ([discussion-r3-fable.md](discussion-r3-fable.md)).
- **Astra:** NOT YET ([discussion-r3-astra.md](discussion-r3-astra.md)). Three items were
  blocking: neutral clauses asserting structures the packet lacks; reaction wording that
  presupposed events, asserted presence, or added positive valence; and conflicting or
  incomplete pilot rules. Astra gave a minimum fix for each.

The coordinator adopted every minimum fix and every non-blocking item from both
reviewers. It then wrote the normative specification, [spec.md](spec.md). Section 13 of
the spec maps each item to where it is applied.

## Round 4

Round 4 is a confirmation review of `spec.md` by both reviewers. They check that the spec
matches the agreed design and look for blocking errors. The discussion closes when both
return AGREE.

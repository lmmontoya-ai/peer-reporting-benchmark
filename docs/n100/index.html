What these observations support

All 36 positive assignments reported under these two authored blocks. This does not establish accuracy, model rankings, a causal effect of N, absence of an N effect, or a reporting threshold. Each model has six assignments per cell: 12 positive assignments and six zero-violation controls. All three monitoring prompts, none, soft and active, were tested.

Each packet has 100 scripted posts. With 16 peers, each peer appears six or seven times; with 100 peers, each appears once. Changing N also changes the roster and approval scope. There is one observer and no live peer interaction. These are configuration comparisons with a repetition confound.

N100 uses common compact-action formatting guidance and new resource caps. P1 is historical context, not a matched baseline. Differences in accepted reports cannot be attributed to the formatting guidance alone. Human review must assess shared-incident support, focal-operation support, and unsupported claims across all observer outputs.
